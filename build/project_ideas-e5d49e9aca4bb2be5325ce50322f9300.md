# Project Ideas

During the project phase, you will work in groups of 2-3 students and use your coding skills to solve a real-world problem of your choice. Below are some ideas for projects. Feel free to come up with your own ideas!

## Predicting ideal TLC mobile phase composition for organic molecules

Identifying the ideal mixture of the mobile phase in thin-layer chromatography (TLC) can be tricky without experience, but is crucial for successful separation. In this project, you will curate a small dataset of organic molecules, train a simple regression model to predict the ideal TLC mobile phase composition, and evaluate its performance on our test set. This project touches on chapters {ref}`sec:regression` and molecular representation.
