# Neural Networks

In this final chapter, we will explore *neural networks*, a special class of machine learning (ML) algorithms that have become particularly popular in the last decade. This subfield of machine learning is also known as *deep learning*, referring to ML with *deep neural networks*.

Deep learning is largely responsible for the current AI hype and has led to significant advances in many applications (e.g., ChatGPT in natural language processing, Stable Diffusion in image generation, and AlphaFold in protein structure prediction). The fundamental idea of neural networks was developed as early as the 1950s, but has only become popular in recent years due to advances in hardware, software, and data availability.
